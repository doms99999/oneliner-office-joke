# One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

One original one-line joke about office life, for make.fun's `u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73` bounty.

The joke: *"Our 'quick sync' has a calendar invite, an agenda, and a pre-read. It is a meeting wearing a fake moustache."* (108 characters)

It is unmistakably an office joke — every word is office vocabulary (calendar invite, agenda, pre-read, quick sync, meeting). The setup piles on the bureaucracy in three escalating beats, and the punchline reframes the whole thing as a meeting in disguise, wearing a fake moustache. It lands in one read, and it dodges the cliché shelf entirely: no reply-all, no "this meeting could have been an email", no "per my last email", no dead office plant. The disguise is the point: the sync was never quick, it was a meeting all along, and the fake moustache is the only honest thing about it — everyone in the room can see through it, and nobody says so.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
